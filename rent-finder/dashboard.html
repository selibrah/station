What runs in the background

    
Without this page, in Terminal


cd ~/Desktop/gemini-station-workspace/rent-finder
python3 dashboard.py --jobs     # what is running
python3 dashboard.py --stop     # stop the watcher and its browser
python3 dashboard.py --start    # turn them back on
python3 dashboard.py            # this page, at http://127.0.0.1:8787

      
Stopping deletes nothing: the listings, your notes, the rules and the Facebook sign-in stay as they are. To take the hosted copy off the web as well, remove the dar-radar project in Vercel.
