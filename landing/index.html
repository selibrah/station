Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on architecture, security, and daily workflows.

How does Station work without a Chrome extension?

Station launches or attaches to Chrome with an isolated --user-data-dir profile (`~/.flossi/profiles/<name>`) and maintains a persistent Rust WebSocket directly over the Chrome DevTools Protocol. You log into LinkedIn, GitHub, or Facebook once in that profile, and Station reuses it across restarts with zero Manifest V3 extension limits.

Will background tabs interrupt my work or steal window focus?

No. Both station_tabs (MCP) and Auto-Jobs create tabs via Target.createTarget with background: true and pool persistent secondary WebSockets—so agents read pages and extract data behind the scenes while you keep coding.

How does Station keep my logged-in browser secure?

Station binds strictly to loopback (127.0.0.1:19826), omits CORS headers so malicious websites cannot query the daemon from your browser, blocks file: / javascript: navigation schemes, and requires a constant-time verified 256-bit token stored at mode 0600.

Does Station bypass CAPTCHAs or spam job applications?

Never. Station is built for human-in-the-loop power: if a site presents a CAPTCHA or login check, Station stops cleanly and alerts you. For job applications, the authenticated_assist workflow pre-fills forms and attaches your PDF resume, pausing before the final Submit click so you stay in control.
